How to Play

From zero to mining in five steps.

1. SET UP YOUR WALLET

Install an EVM wallet extension (e.g. MetaMask or Rabby), then add the Robinhood Chain network this deployment runs on:

You need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain for gas and chest purchases.

2. CONNECT AND SIGN IN

Click Connect wallet on the game screen, approve the connection, then sign the login message (free — it is a signature, not a transaction). The wallet chip in the header shows your address; click it any time to disconnect.

3. GET HEROES

Open the SHOP and buy a chest (0.005 ETH) or the 10-pack (0.04 ETH, 20% off). After buying, the chest-opening screen appears: wait ~30 seconds while the chest is prepared, press OPEN CHEST, confirm in your wallet and watch the reveal — the rarity colors tell you how lucky you got. New heroes appear on the mining screen within a minute.

4. MINE AND MANAGE STAMINA
  • Click a hero → Work to start mining. Each bomb breaks ore and earns $BLAST.
  • Stamina drains while mining; at zero the hero rests automatically.
  • Buy a house in the Shop (paid in $BLAST) and use Shelter on a hero to speed up recovery.
  • Try Adventure for bigger one-shot rewards — success scales with hero power, 10 tries per day.
  • See your full roster on the HEROES page in the header.
5. WITHDRAW YOUR $BLAST

Your earnings accumulate in the pill counter at the top of the mining screen. Once you reach the minimum shown in-game (30,000 $BLAST at launch, with a 24-hour cooldown between withdrawals), the Claim button unlocks: press it, confirm the transaction, and the tokens are transferred to your wallet on-chain.

TIPS
  • Higher-rarity heroes mine faster, hit harder and have far more stamina.
  • Fusing two same-rarity heroes levels one up (+10% attributes per level).
  • Blocks show cracks as they get close to breaking; harder ore pays more.
  • During beta phases on testnet, all rewards and NFTs are test assets with no real value.
