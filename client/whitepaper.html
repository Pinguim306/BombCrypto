Whitepaper

MinerBlast — an explosive play-to-earn mining game built on Robinhood Chain.

1. OVERVIEW

MinerBlast is a 2D pixel-art idle mining game. Players collect miner hero NFTs that dig through ore blocks with bombs, earning $BLAST — the game's utility token. All game logic that decides rewards runs on an authoritative server, and every asset (heroes, houses, rewards) settles on-chain on Robinhood Chain.

2. HEROES

Heroes are ERC-721 NFTs with on-chain attributes: Power (mining damage), Speed (bombing rate), Stamina (energy), blast range, bomb count and special abilities. There are six rarity tiers:

Common — 52% Rare — 26% Super Rare — 12% Epic — 6.5% Legend — 3% Mythic — 0.5%

Heroes come from chests bought in the Shop with ETH. The odds above are published on-chain in the gacha contract. Heroes can be fused (burning a same-rarity sacrifice plus a $BLAST fee) to level up, and traded on the in-game Market.

3. GAME MODES
ModeDescription
Treasure MiningIdle mode: heroes mine ore blocks automatically, spending stamina and earning $BLAST — even while the game is closed.
AdventureSend a hero on an expedition (3 stages of rising difficulty). Success chance scales with hero power; up to 10 attempts per day.
HousesStamina houses (ERC-721) shelter heroes so they recover energy faster. Bought with $BLAST.
MarketPlayer-to-player trading of heroes and houses, paid in $BLAST with a 4% fee (half of it burned).
4. THE $BLAST TOKEN
  • Fixed supply of 1 billion, issued via the pons launchpad on Robinhood Chain — a plain ERC-20 the game contracts can never mint.
  • The reward vault is pre-funded: the project deposits a share of supply, and creator fees from the token's trading pool (70% of the 1% pool fee) keep topping it up.
  • All in-game burns (gacha share, house purchases, upgrade fees, market fee share) go to the dead address 0x…dEaD.
  • Chest revenue in ETH goes to the treasury, which buys $BLAST back to refill the vault.
5. EARNING AND WITHDRAWING

Rewards accrue in-game and are withdrawn on-chain with a server-signed voucher (EIP-712). Withdrawal rules protect the economy:

RuleValue at launch
Minimum per withdrawal30,000 $BLAST
Cooldown between withdrawals24 hours
Global daily payout capAdmin-tuned from live economy data

Beta phases on testnet run with reduced limits so testers can exercise the full flow quickly; the in-game HUD always shows the live values.
