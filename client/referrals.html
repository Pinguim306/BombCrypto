How it works

  1. Connect your wallet and copy your referral link above.
  2. Share it. Anyone who opens the game through your link is tied to you on their first chest purchase — permanently.
  3. Every chest (single or 10-pack) they ever buy pays you 15% of the ETH, on the spot.
  4. Payouts arrive straight in your wallet. If a transfer can't be delivered automatically, it waits here as claimable ETH.

Self-referral (buying through your own link) pays nothing — it just goes to the treasury as usual.
