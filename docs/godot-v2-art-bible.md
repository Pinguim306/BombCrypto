# MinerBlast v2 — art bible

Brief for whoever produces the final art for the Godot client: a pixel artist,
or the owner driving an AI art tool (PixelLab, Retro Diffusion, Midjourney +
Aseprite clean-up). The **placeholder set** already in the repo
(`godot/assets/art/`, generated by `pnpm godot:art`) shows every file at its
final size and frame count; the final art **replaces files by name** and the
game picks them up without code changes. `godot/tools/art/CONTRACT.md` is the
exact file list; this document is the *why* and the *look*.

Legal line: MinerBlast is its own game. Nothing may be traced, copied or
"closely inspired" from Bomb Crypto or any other title — silhouettes,
headgear, props and UI ornaments must be original. Deliverables must be
licensed for commercial use by MinerBlast.

## 1. The look in one paragraph

Chunky, saturated, slightly cartoon **pixel art at 1×** for a 960×540 canvas
that browsers scale up. Every sprite has a **1 px dark outline**
(`#10141f`). Light comes from the **top-left**; shadows are cool
(navy-shifted), highlights warm (cream-shifted). Heroes are chibi: big head
(≈45 % of height), short legs, expressive 2 px eyes with a white highlight.
The cave is dark navy-brown so heroes, gems and gold pop; the HUD is wood,
brass and gold with a red ribbon for titles. Readable at 1× in a browser tab,
appealing at 3× in a screenshot.

## 2. Palette

| role | hex |
|---|---|
| outline | `#10141f` |
| cave floor | `#1c1a2a` → `#2a2436` (low contrast, pebbles ±8 %) |
| rock wall | `#5d4a3a` `#7a6350` `#8d6e63` (top-left lit) |
| stone block (tier 0) | `#8d6e63` + gold nuggets `#ffca28` / `#fff3b0` |
| ice block (tier 1) | `#78909c` + cyan crystals `#4fc3f7` / `#b3ecff` |
| basalt block (tier 2) | `#3b3040` + magma `#ff7043` / `#ffca28` |
| wood | `#8b5a2b` `#a0673a` `#c98b4b` (grain 1 px darker) |
| brass / gold | `#b8860b` `#ffca28` `#fff3b0` |
| ribbon red | `#c62828` `#8e1b1b`, gold trim `#ffca28` |
| rarity 0..5 | `#9e9e9e` `#66bb6a` `#42a5f5` `#ab47bc` `#ffa726` `#ef5350` |
| UI text | `#eceff1` body, `#ffca28` numbers, `#90a4ae` muted, `#a5d6a7` success, `#ef9a9a` error |

Keep the whole set inside ~48 colours; use the ramp idea (dark2 / dark /
base / light / light2) per material rather than free gradients.

## 3. Heroes (`chars/hero_<rarity>.png`, 32×32 × 11 frames)

Strip order and timing (fixed by the manifest, can be extended there):

| anim | frames | fps | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle | 0–1 | 2 | 1 px breathing bob |
| walk | 2–5 | 8 | contact / passing / contact / passing; lifted foot 2 px up; arms counter-swing; body 1 px up on passing frames |
| throw | 6–8 | 10 | wind-up (arm back, bomb in hand), release (arm forward, bomb gone, mouth open), recover (slight crouch) |
| sleep | 9–10 | 1.5 | sitting, knees up, eyes as 1 px lines, 1 px chest rise |

Rules: faces **right** (the game flips for left); feet on **y = 30**, centred
on **x = 16**, nothing but outline on rows 0 and 31; the bomb the hero holds
is a 7 px version of `bomb.png`. Same body for all six; **rarity shows in the
outfit colour and the headgear**:

| rarity | outfit | headgear / signature |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Common | grey | brown cloth cap with brim |
| 1 Rare | green | bandana with knot tail + cyan goggles on the forehead |
| 2 Super Rare | blue | mining helmet with a lit head-lamp (the lamp is a real light in-game) |
| 3 Epic | purple | hood with two small bone horns |
| 4 Legendary | orange/gold | gold crown with a red gem, red cape, belt pouch |
| 5 Mythic | red | demon horns + flame hair (animated flicker welcome in idle) |

Also: `portrait_<rarity>.png` 32×32 bust (head ≈70 % of the frame), same
headgear; `hero_shadow.png` 20×8 soft ellipse.

## 4. Props and effects (`chars/`)

* `bomb.png` 24×24 ×2 — round black bomb, grey highlight top-left, short fuse
  top-right, spark on / off.
* `boom.png` 64×64 ×6 — cartoon explosion: white star flash → yellow/orange
  puffs → red with smoke curling → grey smoke with holes → thin dissipating
  smoke. Irregular puffs, never perfect circles.
* `coin.png` 16×16 ×6 spin (edge-on at frame 3), `coin_big.png` 32×32 for the
  HUD, both with a "B".
* `chest.png` / `chest_open.png` 32×32, `campfire.png` 32×32 ×4,
  `torch.png` 16×32 ×4 (flames change shape every frame), `zzz.png` 16×16 ×3,
  `smoke_puff.png` 12×12.

## 5. Environment (`env/`)

The map is 12×8 tiles of 48 px in a **3/4 top-down view**. The 40 server
blocks are **ore deposits scattered over the cave floor** (the pattern lives
in `godot/tools/art/map.mjs`); heroes walk up to them and throw.

* `ore_{tier}_{variant}.png` 48×48 ×3 frames — an organic mound sitting on
  the floor (irregular silhouette, soft ground shadow, never a cube):
  tier 0 stone + gold nuggets, tier 1 ice + cyan crystal cluster, tier 2
  basalt + glowing magma grooves; two silhouette variants per tier. Frame 0
  intact, frame 1 cracked with small chips, frame 2 heavily damaged (bigger
  bites, gems gone, rubble at the base) but still recognisable.
* `floor.png` 48×48 ×4 variants (low contrast), `floor_crystal.png` (floor
  with a glowing crystal cluster: shown where a depleted block reveals a
  vein), `crater.png` (scorched decal for depleted blocks).
* `map_bg.png` 576×384 — the baked floor with a **12 px irregular rock
  border** and sparse rocks / mushrooms / bones on tiles that hold no
  deposit.
* `wall_band.png` 576×28 — rock wall with stalactites, drawn above the map.
* `house.png` 96×80 — log cabin, red tiled roof with ridge, chimney top-right
  (smoke particles spawn there), door with knob, warm window (a light sits
  there). Manifest `extra: { window: [x, y], chimney: [x, y] }`.
* small props: `rock_0/1` 24×20, `mushroom` 16×16, `bones` 24×12,
  `crystal_0/1` 24×32 (cyan / purple), `stalactite` 16×24, `sign` 32×24.

## 6. UI (`ui/`)

All panels and buttons are **9-slice** PNGs: the margins carry the detail,
the centre is a plain repeatable fill (it gets stretched).

* `panel_wood.png` 48×48 (margins 12) — 3–4 horizontal planks with grain and
  seams, dark rim, brass rivets in the corners. Used for the top bar, side
  panels, bottom bar and the hero card.
* `panel_dark.png` 48×48 (margins 8) — navy inset for lists and stats.
* `frame_gold.png` 48×48 (margins 12) — ornate bevelled gold frame,
  transparent centre (selected stage, popup portrait).
* `plate.png` 48×32 (margins 10) — dark rounded plate with a gold rim
  (the BLAST and rate counters).
* `btn_<green|blue|red|gray|gold|teal>.png` 48×40 (margins 12/12/12/16) —
  rounded face, top highlight, **4 px darker bottom edge**;
  `btn_<colour>_down.png` — face moved 3 px down, edge 1 px.
* `ribbon.png` 96×32 (margins 24) — red banner, gold trim, folded darker tails.
* `portrait_frame.png` 40×40 (transparent 32×32 centre at 4,4), `bar_frame.png`
  24×10, `badge.png` 20×20 red round badge.
* icons 16×16: pick, coin, bolt, house, bomb, star, lock, gift, adventure,
  arrow_l, arrow_r, close, sleep, wallet.
* `logo.png` 206×40 and `logo_small.png` 144×32 — the lockup: the bomb on
  the left, a breath of space, then "MINERBLAST" in chunky gold bevelled
  letters with the outline. No other accents; the same lockup is used on the
  site header (2×), the connect screen (3×) and the in-game title.

Fonts are not art deliverables: Press Start 2P (titles, numbers, buttons) and
Pixelify Sans (body), both OFL, already in `godot/assets/fonts/`.

## 7. Delivery checklist

1. PNG, RGBA, **1× pixel scale** (no upscaled files), transparent background.
2. Exact sizes and frame counts from `CONTRACT.md`; horizontal strips, frame
   `i` at `x = i * w`. Need more frames? Say so — it is a one-line manifest edit.
3. One outline colour, one light direction, one palette across the set.
4. Heroes: feet on y = 30, centred x = 16, facing right.
5. 9-slice files: plain centre, detail in the margins; test by stretching to
   300×100 and 100×300.
6. Drop the files into `godot/assets/art/<sub>/`, run `pnpm godot:test` (the
   smoke test loads every sprite) and `pnpm godot:export`, then look at the
   web build (`/play2.html?mock=1`).

## 8. Prompts for AI art tools (starting points)

Use the palette and sizes above; generate large, then downscale + clean in
Aseprite (index to the palette, redraw the outline, fix symmetry). Never feed
another game's art as a reference image.

* Hero (per rarity): "32x32 pixel art chibi miner, big head, short legs, 1px
  dark outline, {outfit colour} overalls, {headgear}, small backpack, facing
  right, idle pose, top-left lighting, transparent background, 16 colours".
* Ore block: "48x48 pixel art cube in 3/4 top-down view, lighter top face 14
  px tall, front face with {gold nuggets | cyan ice crystals | glowing magma
  cracks}, bevelled edges, 1px dark outline, transparent background".
* Cabin: "96x80 pixel art log cabin, red clay tile roof with ridge, stone
  chimney top-right, wooden door with knob, warm lit window, cute
  proportions, 1px outline, transparent background".
* UI panel: "48x48 pixel art nine-slice wooden plank panel, dark rim, brass
  corner rivets, seamless centre".
* Logo: "round black pixel bomb with a lit fuse on the left, then chunky gold
  bevelled pixel letters MINERBLAST with a dark outline, transparent
  background, 206x40".
